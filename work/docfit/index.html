DocFit

Rethinking document generation with AI.

AI Product · Document Automation · B2B SaaS

Role
Solo Product Designer
Team
Product Designer + Development Team

00The 30-second version

Problem
Businesses often use mail merge to generate documents from spreadsheets and fixed templates. However, formatting issues, data changes and manual page-by-page checks make the process time-consuming and difficult to manage at scale.
My role
As the solo Product Designer, I designed DocFit from scratch, owning product strategy, information architecture, user flows, UI and interactive prototyping.
Key decisions
I designed AI-assisted field mapping with user confirmation, automated document validation to identify formatting issues, and a conversational AI correction experience that lets users fix individual or multiple pages while retaining control through suggestions, undo and version history.

About product

01

The challenge

Many organisations rely on spreadsheets to maintain data and fixed templates to generate documents.

Context

For example, schools use student spreadsheets and board-provided templates to create thousands of hall tickets. HR teams generate employee documents, while other industries produce standardised reports and records.

Traditional mail merge helps transfer data into templates, but struggles with real-world variations.

Key problems

  • Layout inconsistencies

    Long names and varying data lengths can disrupt alignment and page layouts.

  • Manual verification

    Administrators have to inspect individual pages to find formatting issues.

  • Time-consuming corrections

    Changes to source data often require repeating the merge process and checking the generated documents again.

  • Poor scalability

    Managing thousands of documents makes manual corrections increasingly difficult.

In one school scenario, seven classes with approximately 300 students each could mean generating and checking around 2,100 hall tickets.

The opportunity

Use AI to automate not just data merging, but also document validation and repetitive corrections.

02

What research showed

I spoke to school heads and to HR people at a few companies, and I had seen the same thing at places I’d worked myself.

It wasn’t only schools. I found the same pattern in HR, finance, healthcare and education: people fitting spreadsheet data into a set document by hand.

The trouble starts after the data goes in:

  1. 01

    Text wraps onto a new line

  2. 02

    A table shifts, a page breaks in the wrong place

  3. 03

    In a batch of hundreds, fixing one page means checking whether the same thing happened on all the others

The tools people already use, from mail merge to general AI assistants, each solve part of this. None of them keeps a layout reliable across a big batch.

03

Product strategy

I defined the information architecture, navigation and end-to-end user journey around one central goal:

The goal

Make document generation, review and correction faster without taking control away from the user.

What I left out

I deliberately kept template creation and editing, along with team collaboration, outside the initial scope. This allowed the product to focus on doing one thing well: merging existing data into existing templates and handling the problems that arise.

04

Key design decisions

Design decisions

Design focusWhat I designedWhy it mattered
AI-assisted field mapping AI matches spreadsheet columns with template fields. Users can review, edit, add or remove mappings, with uncertain matches highlighted for confirmation. Reduces manual mapping while keeping users in control of data accuracy.
Automated document validation AI identifies alignment changes, overflowing text and unexpected page shifts in generated documents. Reduces the effort of manually inspecting every page.
Instruction-based corrections Users can request changes through an AI chat panel, accept suggested fixes or apply instructions across multiple pages. Replaces repetitive page-by-page corrections with reusable instructions.

05

The core product experience

Rather than showcasing every module, I’m highlighting three key moments that communicate how DocFit works.

01 Uploading data

Users start by uploading their spreadsheet and document template. DocFit analyses the files and prepares the data for mapping.

02 Reviewing generated documents

Once the data is mapped and documents are generated, users can preview the results, navigate through pages and identify areas that need attention.

03 Correcting documents with AI

Users can interact with AI through a dedicated chat panel, describe what needs to be changed and review suggested corrections before applying them. Changes can be made to individual pages or across multiple pages.

The guiding principle

AI handles repetitive work. Users stay in control.

The complete workflow

  1. Upload data & template
  2. AI-assisted field mapping
  3. Generate documents
  4. Review & detect issues
  5. Correct with AI
  6. Export & save

06

Beyond the showcased experience

The product also includes supporting capabilities designed to manage the complete document lifecycle.

Supporting capabilities

  • Editable field mapping

    Review, modify and confirm AI-suggested data mappings.

  • Document validation

    Detect formatting inconsistencies, overflowing text and layout changes.

  • Version management

    Revisit previous versions, undo changes and restore documents.

  • Workspace management

    Save projects as drafts, update source data and regenerate documents.

  • Export and sharing

    Download PDFs or editable documents and share through Google Drive or links.

Due to NDA restrictions, these additional workflows and screens are not shown in this case study.

07

Intended impact

DocFit is designed to help organisations:

  • Reduce manual effort involved in generating and checking large batches of documents.

  • Improve document consistency by identifying formatting and layout issues before export.

  • Handle data changes efficiently without repeating the entire manual correction process.

  • Scale document workflows across larger batches while retaining user oversight.

The product’s core experience and interactive prototype are complete, and the development team is working towards implementation.
