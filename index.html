A designer by curiosity.

When campus placements rolled around, most of my classmates were heading into development. I wanted to take a different route.

Engineering taught me to think in systems. Design gave me a way to question them.

I started exploring how people think, why products work the way they do, and how small decisions can change an entire experience. That curiosity led me to product design, and it hasn’t really stopped since.
