A little about how I think about design.

Coming from a computer engineering background, I’ve always been curious about how digital products work, what goes into building them, why they matter and the problems they solve.

I believe good design starts with the right problem, balancing user needs, business goals and technical constraints to create meaningful solutions.

What I enjoy most is working with people across disciplines. Different perspectives from product, engineering and research often lead to better questions and stronger solutions.

For me, design doesn’t stop at shipping. It’s about learning from feedback, understanding what works and continuously improving the experience.
